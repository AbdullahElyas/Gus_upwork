The Right foot: The right foot presented in a neutral position, with the centre of mass appropriately aligned over the 2nd metatarsal. Foot and Ankle summary: There is clear asymmetry between the feet, particularly in dorsiflexion and plantarflexion force and range, with the left foot more compromised overall. Both feet lack functional pronation despite theoretical capability, and both demonstrate poor plantarflexion strength and range, indicating a need for targeted intervention. Prioritise overcoming isometric strength work, especially for plantarflexion and dorsiflexion, and incorporate midfoot articulation drills to enhance fascial control and proprioceptive awareness. Subconscious movement strategies, such as dynamic gait retraining and barefoot balance work, should be introduced to encourage tissue lengthening and improve automatic control. Regular reassessment will be essential to monitor progress and adjust the strengthening programme accordingly.
